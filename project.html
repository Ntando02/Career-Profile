Campus Queue Management System

CampusQueue is a web-based system designed to help students join, monitor and manage campus service queues remotely. It aims to reduce long physical queues and give students a better idea of their position and waiting time.

Services

01

Registration

Join the registration queue online.

02

Financial Aid

Manage financial aid service queues.

03

Academic Advising

Access academic support more easily.

04

Administration

Join general administration queues.

Key Features

  • Student registration and login
  • Online queue joining
  • Automatic ticket generation
  • Real-time queue position
  • Admin dashboard
  • Calling the next student
